# GC-R010 — Third-Party Evidence Linking

## Statement

The system shall support linking evidence artifacts to third-party assessments, including SOC 2 reports, ISO certifications, penetration test results, insurance certificates, and questionnaire responses. Evidence shall have expiration dates and the system shall flag expired evidence.

## Rationale

Vendor due diligence produces evidence that must be tracked, version-controlled, and monitored for expiration. Linking evidence to vendor assessments closes the audit loop — auditors can trace from vendor risk score to the underlying evidence.
