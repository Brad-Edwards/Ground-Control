# GC-K003 — Policy Violation Reporting

## Statement

The system shall report policy violations linking the violated policy, the failing evaluation, the affected requirements, and the non-compliant artifacts, enabling targeted remediation.

## Rationale

Violation reports without context are actionless. Linking violations to policies, requirements, and artifacts tells the developer exactly what is wrong, why it matters, and what needs to change.
