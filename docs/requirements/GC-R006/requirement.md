# GC-R006 — Questionnaire Scoring Engine

## Statement

The system shall support automated scoring of questionnaire responses based on template-defined rubrics, including weighted scoring, pass/fail thresholds, section-level scores, and overall risk score derivation. Scoring shall be deterministic and auditable.

## Rationale

Manual scoring of vendor questionnaires is slow and inconsistent. Automated scoring enables agents to process vendor assessments at scale and immediately update vendor risk profiles based on questionnaire results.
