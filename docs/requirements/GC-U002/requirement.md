# GC-U002 — Audit Workpaper Management

## Statement

The system shall support workpaper entities linked to audits: working documents, analysis notes, evidence references, and review status (prepared, reviewed, approved). Workpapers shall maintain audit evidence chain of custody (who prepared, who reviewed, when).

## Rationale

Workpapers are the documented basis for audit conclusions. Chain of custody ensures audit evidence integrity. This is a regulatory requirement for internal audit functions (IIA Standards) and external audits (PCAOB).
