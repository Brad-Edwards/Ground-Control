# GC-T015 — NIST Risk Response and Ongoing Monitoring

## Statement

The system shall support NIST-compatible risk response planning and ongoing monitoring by recording response approach, response rationale, monitored risk factors, update cadence, and reassessment triggers derived from changes in threats, vulnerabilities, predisposing conditions, operational assets, topology, controls, observations, or environment. Response planning shall support accept, avoid, mitigate, transfer or share, and other organization-defined variants while preserving traceability to the originating risk assessment.

## Rationale

NIST guidance does not stop at assessment; it expects risk response and assessment updates informed by ongoing monitoring. In a graph-native system, monitored changes include asset, topology, and observation changes as well as threats and controls.
