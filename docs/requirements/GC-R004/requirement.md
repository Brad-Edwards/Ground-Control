# GC-R004 — Questionnaire Template Entity

## Statement

The system shall support a questionnaire template entity with sections, questions (text, single-select, multi-select, numeric, file-upload), scoring rubrics per question, and version tracking. Templates shall be reusable across multiple third parties and assessment campaigns.

## Rationale

Questionnaires are the primary data collection mechanism in TPRM and compliance assessments. Agents need structured templates to generate, distribute, and programmatically score questionnaire responses — replacing manual PDF-based vendor assessment workflows.
