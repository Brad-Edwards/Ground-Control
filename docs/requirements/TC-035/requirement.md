# TC-035 — Cross-Project Reports

## Statement

The system shall support reporting across multiple projects with aggregated metrics, comparative analysis, and consolidated dashboards.

## Rationale

TestRail Enterprise, Zephyr Scale, and TestLink support cross-project reporting. Essential for organizations managing multiple products or teams from a single instance.
