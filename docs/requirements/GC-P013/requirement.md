# GC-P013 — Graph-Native GRC Reporting and Export

## Statement

The system shall support exporting graph-native GRC reports and evidence packages for different audiences, including analysts, engineers, auditors, and executives, with outputs grounded in the underlying graph and explicit about methodology when FAIR, NIST, or ISO-derived assessments are included.

## Rationale

Not every consumer of risk and assurance information works inside Ground Control. Exports and report packages need to preserve graph context and methodology provenance rather than flattening everything into ambiguous summary tables.
