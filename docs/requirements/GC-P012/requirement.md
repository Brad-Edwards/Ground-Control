# GC-P012 — Graph-Native GRC Import and Interchange

## Statement

The system shall support importing and exporting graph-native GRC data for operational assets, topology, risk scenarios, control catalogs, findings, evidence references, and assessments using structured interchange formats and mapping workflows that preserve identifiers, provenance, timestamps, and typed relations.

## Rationale

A graph-native factory cannot rely on manual re-entry for assets, scenarios, controls, or evidence context. Import and interchange capabilities are required to bring external inventories and assurance data into the shared graph without destroying traceability semantics.
