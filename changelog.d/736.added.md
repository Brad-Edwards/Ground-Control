Add executable-evidence source kinds for GC-I003: `CI_PIPELINE_RESULT` and
`SECURITY_SCAN_RESULT` carry opaque, length-capped identifiers (never
dereferenced server-side) and route through the existing external-mode
validation path. Existing internal kinds `CONTROL_TEST` and
`VERIFICATION_RESULT` already cover executable test/proof evidence per
ADR-014 and ADR-039; no new abstraction was needed for them. ADR-045 §8
amendment.
