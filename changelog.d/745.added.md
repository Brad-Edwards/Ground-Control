### Added - FAIR materiality and loss taxonomy support (GC-T016)

The read-only FAIR quantitative analysis envelope (`GET /api/v1/analysis/grc/fair-quantitative`) now expresses a **materiality** view under `outputs.materiality` built from two distinct, separately sourced FAIR taxonomies: a typed decomposition of the `fair_mam` loss magnitude into the ten **FAIR Materiality Assessment Model (FAIR-MAM)** primary cost modules (`FairMamCostModule`, FAIR Institute) with a summed total, plus stakeholder-specific secondary effects classified by the six **FAIR forms of loss** from The Open Group Risk Taxonomy (`FairFormOfLoss`, O-RT) via an optional `secondary_loss_by_stakeholder` input. The view is descriptive only and never alters the canonical ALE derivation. Cost modules and stakeholder magnitudes reuse the existing FAIR three-point invariant and currency-consistency checks (mismatched or invalid entries are excluded with a `limitations` note). The FAIR_V3_0 methodology profile schema documents the new input/output vocabulary (migration `V138`).
