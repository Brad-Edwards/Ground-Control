### Added - FAIR materiality and loss taxonomy support (GC-T016)

The read-only FAIR quantitative analysis envelope (`GET /api/v1/analysis/grc/fair-quantitative`) now expresses a FAIR-MAM **materiality** view under `outputs.materiality`: a typed decomposition of the `fair_mam` loss-magnitude breakdown into the six FAIR forms of loss (`FairLossForm`) with a summed total, plus stakeholder-specific secondary effects from an optional `secondary_loss_by_stakeholder` input. The view is descriptive only and never alters the canonical ALE derivation. Loss forms and stakeholder magnitudes reuse the existing FAIR three-point invariant and currency-consistency checks (mismatched or invalid entries are excluded with a `limitations` note). The FAIR_V3_0 methodology profile schema documents the new input/output vocabulary (migration `V138`).
