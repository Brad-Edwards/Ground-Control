### Added

- **Risk Appetite & Tolerance Profiles (GC-T005)**: Versioned, project-scoped
  `RiskAppetiteProfile` aggregate carrying methodology-appropriate tolerance
  bands—qualitative criteria, FAIR-style monetary ranges, loss event
  frequency caps, and exceedance probability caps. A new
  `RiskAppetiteEvaluator` kernel under `domain/riskscenarios/service/`
  classifies risk values (`WITHIN` / `APPROACHING` / `EXCEEDS` /
  `NOT_EVALUATED`) and is the shared substrate consumed by the campaign
  evaluation phase (GC-T006) and KRI breach analytics (GC-T007).
  CRUD lives at `/api/v1/risk-appetite-profiles`; activating a new version
  archives any prior `active` version under the same `profileKey`.
- New `AppetiteToleranceKind` enum (`QUALITATIVE`, `MONETARY_RANGE`,
  `LOSS_EVENT_FREQUENCY`, `EXCEEDANCE_PROBABILITY`, `COMPOSITE`),
  mirrored at the frontend (`APPETITE_TOLERANCE_KINDS`) and MCP boundaries
  per ADR-034.
